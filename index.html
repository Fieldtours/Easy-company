How the guides work

1

Plan. Tell the Tour planner how many days you have; it builds the route and the overnight stops.

2

Go. Every stop has a Google Maps button, and the Map shows all your stops at once.

3

Read. The Timeline, The Men and Origins tell the story; the bar at the bottom of the screen always takes you home.

No signal? No problem

Each guide can be saved to your phone and used with no internet. Open a guide, tap More at the bottom, then Save for offline — do it on Wi-Fi before you travel. Tip: add this site to your home screen and it opens like an app.
